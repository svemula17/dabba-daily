FAQ

Common questions.

Are we in your neighborhood?
Delivery is free in Plainsboro (08536). Nearby ZIPs like West Windsor (08550), Princeton (08540), Cranbury (08512) and Monroe (08831) can order with a delivery fee.
Do meal packs renew automatically?
No. You pay once for the pack and use the meals on any days you choose before it expires. No subscription, no auto-renewal.
What if I'm travelling?
Just don't order on the days you're away. A 10-meal pack can be used within 21 days, so you can be away a few days and still use every meal.
Can I mix South and North Indian meals?
Yes. Pick a cuisine for each day when you order. Both cost the same and count as one meal from your pack.
Is delivery free?
Yes, in Plainsboro (08536). Nearby ZIPs such as West Windsor, Princeton, Cranbury and Monroe pay a delivery fee per order.
Where is the food cooked?
In a licensed, health-inspected restaurant kitchen in Plainsboro, by trained chefs, using the oils we list on every lid.
Will my food arrive hot?
We pack at 165°F into preheated insulated carriers, and every run stays under 30 minutes. Packs of 20 meals or more come in an insulated steel hot box that keeps the whole meal warm.
How does the steel hot box deposit work?
Packs of 20 meals or more are delivered in an insulated steel hot box. You pay a $50 refundable deposit with your pack. Leave the empty box out and our driver swaps it for a full one on your next delivery day.
Is the packaging eco-friendly?
Yes. Containers are certified compostable, with sealed cups for dal and curries so nothing leaks.
Can I order on delivery apps?
Yes, single meals and curries are on delivery apps. App prices are higher because the apps charge a fee on every order. Meal packs, pre-order prices and free delivery in 08536 are only available when you order directly.
